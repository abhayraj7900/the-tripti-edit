Shipping & returns

Delivery

Orders are usually dispatched within 2–3 working days. Estimated delivery is 4–8 working days, depending on your location.

Shipping fee

Delivery is complimentary above ₹999. A flat ₹79 fee applies to smaller orders.

Returns

Contact us within 3 days of delivery for damaged or incorrect items. Items must remain unused and in their original packaging.

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, enter your delivery details and tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. We will confirm stock and payment with you.

Can I pay with UPI?

Yes. After confirming your order, use the displayed UPI ID or QR code. Cash on delivery is also available for eligible orders.

How do I choose a clothing size?

Each clothing product includes a size selector and general size guidance. Message us if you would like help before ordering.

Can you help me choose a gift?

Absolutely. Tell us the occasion, recipient and budget on WhatsApp, and we will suggest suitable options.
